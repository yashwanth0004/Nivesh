🚨 Fraud / Scam Awareness — धोखाधड़ी से बचें

  • 🔴 Guaranteed returns are a red flag. No investment guarantees profit — यह धोखा हो सकता है।
  • 🔴 Pressure to act fast ("invest now or lose the opportunity") is a manipulation tactic.
  • 🔴 Unknown brokers or apps asking for OTP / Aadhaar — never share these.
  • 🟢 Always check if the broker is registered with SEBI (sebi.gov.in).
  • 🟢 Report fraud to SEBI SCORES or call the National Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930.
